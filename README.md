# Life - Online Therapy Consultation Website

**Life** is a comprehensive online platform designed to connect individuals with professional therapists, providing a safe, confidential, and accessible environment for mental health support. Through **Life**, users can access a variety of therapy services, including personalized consultations with licensed counselors, online assessments to evaluate their mental well-being, and tailored subscription plans to meet different needs. The goal of **Life** is to offer convenient and flexible therapy options that empower users to take control of their mental health from the comfort of their homes. 

The platform is **mobile-friendly** and fully **responsive**, ensuring a seamless experience across all devices, whether on desktop or mobile.

## Features

- **Landing Page:** An engaging and informative landing page that introduces the platform and its services to potential users.
- **Know Your Counselor:** A section providing detailed profiles of available therapists, allowing users to learn more about the professionals they will be interacting with.
- **Online Assessment:** An interactive and easy-to-use online assessment tool designed to help users better understand their mental health.
- **Paid Plans:** The website includes various subscription plans, offering flexibility for users who wish to access additional therapy sessions or services.
- **Information about the Platform:** A dedicated section providing detailed information about Life, its mission, and the benefits of online therapy.
- **Mobile-Friendly/Responsive Design:** The site is designed to be fully responsive, ensuring a smooth user experience across desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

## Tech Stack

The following technologies were used in the development of the Life website:

- **HTML**: Used for creating the basic structure of the website.
- **CSS**: Applied custom styling to enhance the visual appeal of the platform.
- **JavaScript**: Utilized for interactive elements and dynamic behavior across the website.
- **React**: The core JavaScript library used for building the user interface and managing the state of the application.
- **Tailwind CSS**: A utility-first CSS framework used for rapid styling and responsive design.
- **VS Code**: Integrated development environment (IDE) for writing and editing the code.
- **Git & GitHub**: Version control and collaboration tools used for managing the codebase and hosting the repository.
